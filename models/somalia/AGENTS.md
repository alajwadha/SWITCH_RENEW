## Somalia-specific instructions

Somalia work belongs in a separate folder within SWITCH_RENEW and follows all shared rules.

- Define geographic coverage, baseline year, horizon, modeled services, spatial resolution and time sampling.
- Build a Somalia-specific evidence inventory for demand, generation, storage, fuel, networks, finance and renewable resources.
- Do not inherit Kenya parameters without a documented proxy decision.
- Distinguish existing networks, isolated systems, proposed connections and hypothetical infrastructure.
- Keep recorded consumption, unmet demand estimates and future demand scenarios separate.
- Do not interpret low observed consumption as proof of low electricity need.
- Document boundary sources, versions, naming conventions and included/excluded areas neutrally.
- Maintain asset identifiers, capacity, operating status, source dates and location confidence.
- Prevent duplicate counting of projects, sites and constituent units.
- Establish a small transparent baseline before complex extensions.
- Check historical totals and operational behavior against independent evidence where available.
- Test uncertainty that could change investment decisions, including demand, fuel costs, financing, renewable availability and connection timing.
- Preserve the functioning Kenya and tutorial models.
- Place reusable improvements in shared components; keep Somalia-specific data, scenarios and methodological decisions in its folder.
- Treat hydrogen, desalination, detailed household demand, cross-border trade and other additional sectors as separately scoped proposals.
