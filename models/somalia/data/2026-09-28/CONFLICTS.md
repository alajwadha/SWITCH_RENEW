# Conflicts and exclusions from baseline calibration

These are verified discrepancies or incompatibilities, not numerical corrections to the source. The original records remain in the evidence tables. No disputed value is selected automatically for SWITCH.

| ID | Evidence problem | Consequence and treatment | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| C01 | SNBS Abstract 2024 Table 4.5 reports a geothermal/hydropower-heavy electricity mix inconsistent with other Somalia evidence | Quarantined. Actual origin of the table is not established; do not assume it is Kenya's or silently replace it | [SNBS report, printed p43/PDF p56](https://nbs.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Statistical-Abstract-2024.pdf) |
| C02 | Mogadishu port-fuel table lacks a unit, while adjacent prose inconsistently uses ktoe/kilotons | Preserve printed evidence only; no conversion to physical tonnes or national fuel consumption | [SNBS, pp44–45](https://nbs.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Statistical-Abstract-2024.pdf) |
| C03 | AFREC 2023 balance gives electricity-plant input 37.43 + 15.19 = 52.62 ktoe and output 107.70 ktoe | Output/input is approximately 2.05; incompatible energy accounting. Electricity totals excluded from calibration pending correction | [AFREC 2025 report, p104](https://au-afrec.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/Energy%20Balance%202025%20%28EN%29.pdf) |
| C04 | AFREC sector chart and balance imply different commercial/agriculture electricity shares | Do not combine percentages from one with totals from the other | [AFREC, pp103–104](https://au-afrec.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/Energy%20Balance%202025%20%28EN%29.pdf) |

| C06 | A 2026 SNBS fuel-shock brief pairs approximately USD 0.60 to 1.50/L with 108% growth | Levels imply 150%; preserve approximate levels and quarantine percentage; fuel-grade/delivery basis still unclear | [SNBS inflation brief](https://nbs.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Somalias-Inflation-Outlook.pdf) |
