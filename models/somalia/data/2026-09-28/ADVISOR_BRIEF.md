**Consequential uncertainties:** operating versus contracted capacity, storage MW versus MWh, gross generation versus billed energy, unmet demand, hourly load shape, financing, inconsistent energy-balance tables and administrative versions.

| Immediate national interconnected case | Addresses national planning directly | Requires consequential network, load and financing assumptions before validation |
