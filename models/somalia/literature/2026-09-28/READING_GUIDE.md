# A balanced reading route

Read for **question, method, data and limitations**, not just headline results. All sources below have fuller records in the [catalogue](PUBLICATIONS.md). Where the catalogue says selected sections, this review did not inspect every page.

| Order | Topic | Start here | Read it to answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Electricity | [OnSSET pathways,2023 — E05](https://doi.org/10.3390/en16186489) | How do demand, planning horizon and grid assumptions change electrification choices? |
| 2 | Cooking | [Household energy choices — COOK01](https://doi.org/10.32479/ijeep.19826) | What can the household survey actually measure, and how were fuels grouped? |
| 3 | Transport | [Transport data kit,2024 — TF018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.110984) | Which inputs exist, and which are regional proxies or processed values? |
| 4 | Productive uses | [Groundwater assessment,2021 — PU05](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099615012012129914/pdf/P1749940c70d5d09008a3203ad9eeb97d1a.pdf) | What physical constraints must energy-water planning respect? |
| 5 | Electricity | [Somalia starter-kit note,2021 — E18](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-480695/v1) | How can public inputs be organized without disguising their limitations? |
| 6 | Cooking | COOK14 — PROSCAL terminal evaluation ([catalogue/source](PUBLICATIONS.md#cooking-and-biomass)) | What happened to affordability and continued use after programme support? |
| 7 | Transport/governance | [Somalia First BUR,2022 — TF008](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Somalia%20First%20BUR%20report%202022.pdf), pp.105 and 134–135 | What modelling and transport assumptions already existed? |
| 8 | Productive uses | [Hospital hybrid-system study,2021 — PU01](https://doi.org/10.30501/jree.2021.245232.1140) | Which observations support the load, and which outcomes are simulation? |
| 9 | Electricity | [Western Somaliland wind assessment,2024 — E06](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e32500) | How do measurement quality and extrapolation affect resource conclusions? |
| 10 | Cooking/biomass | [Charcoal monitoring,2013 — COOK09](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esd.2013.07.001) | What can imagery establish, and where do conversion assumptions enter? |
| 11 | Transport/governance | [Commodified Cities,2022 — TF009](https://riftvalley.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/rvi-commodified-cities-report-2022-en.pdf), mobility sections | Whose access and constraints disappear from a fuel-only model? |
| 12 | Productive uses | [Somalia WBES 2025 — PU10](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/8119) | What firm-level evidence is available, and which businesses are outside the sample? |

Then read [IRENA's2025 assessment — TF013](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Publication/2025/Jun/IRENA_COU_Energy_transition_assessment_Somalia_2025.pdf) for institutional framing and [SOURCE_ISSUES](SOURCE_ISSUES.md) before extracting numbers. This route deliberately mixes research articles, official evidence and evaluation rather than treating publication type as a quality score.
