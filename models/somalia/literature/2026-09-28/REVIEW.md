# Somalia energy: critical literature synthesis

Reviewed 28 September 2026. **The literature is substantial enough to rule out a broad claim that Somalia lacks energy research. The more defensible contribution is to improve measurement, reproducibility and the connection between energy supply and useful services.** This is a critical scoping review, not an exhaustive systematic review or a new model result.

The [catalogue](PUBLICATIONS.md) contains 73 publication/source records, including one identity-only publication lead and explicitly marked regional context. It covers electricity, cooking/biomass, transport/fuels/governance and productive uses with dedicated searches. The [JSON](publications.json) and [CSV](publications.csv) retain geography, data period, method, provenance, findings, limitations and inspection depth. Publication counts are not counts of independent studies or independent datasets.

## Electricity: move beyond another generic feasibility exercise

Somalia already has spatial electrification planning with OnSSET, illustrative national OSeMOSYS work and official LEAP mitigation analysis. A later electricity-focused LEAP journal article has a more qualified novelty claim that must be assessed against these predecessors. Changing software alone would not establish a research contribution. [E05](https://doi.org/10.3390/en16186489), [E18](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-480695/v1), [TF008](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Somalia%20First%20BUR%20report%202022.pdf), [E11](https://doi.org/10.1002/ese3.70371).

The local hybrid-system literature offers many scenario comparisons. Its modeled costs are not a comparable national cost distribution: appliance loads, diesel prices, financing, grid availability, equipment and unmet-load constraints differ. Some profiles are constructed from appliance assumptions, and some publications have inconsistent demand magnitudes. Reusing their headline costs without rebuilding the cases would conceal these differences. [E01](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2014.07.150), [E02](https://doi.org/10.3390/pr10040667), [E13](https://doi.org/10.30880/ijie.2024.16.01.014).

There are better empirical starting points than a purely synthetic national load: SWALIM station observations, a Mogadishu hospital weather station, a telecom PV case and utility data collected for the electrification plan. These are **data-access leads**, with different years and coverage, rather than a ready calibrated input pack. [E06](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e32500), [E07](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esd.2024.101514), [E03](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esr.2023.101108), [E17](https://moewr.gov.so/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Final-report-for-somalias-least-cost-electrification-.pdf).

**Research opportunity:** test investment decisions against measured service demand, outages, fuel/finance uncertainty and network choices. Start at a resolution the observations can support. An annual renewable-energy share does not itself establish adequate supply during every hour. See the [electricity appraisal](contributions/electricity_review.md).

## Cooking, biomass and charcoal: reconcile definitions and measure continued use

Fuel-choice, spatial, multilevel and machine-learning studies already exist. Several reuse SIHBS 2022 or SHDS 2020; their later publication dates are not new observations of a transition. Different groupings of fuels and residence categories make percentages and coefficients difficult to compare. Survey-derived smoke-risk indicators are also different from measured particulate exposure. [COOK01](https://doi.org/10.32479/ijeep.19826), [COOK02](https://doi.org/10.1177/11786302251315893), [COOK05](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43621-026-03673-0), [COOK18](https://doi.org/10.1177/11786302261427897).

Programme evaluation supplies questions that cross-sectional regressions cannot settle: affordability after subsidy withdrawal, refill access, maintenance and sustained use. Biodigester market potential must also remain separate from the small observed operating base. These are implementation and durability questions, not merely technology availability. [COOK13 and COOK14, source links and locators](PUBLICATIONS.md#cooking-and-biomass).

Charcoal research includes remote sensing and computer vision. Mapping kilns can support monitoring, but converting detections into tonnes of charcoal, trees lost or national forest impacts requires additional assumptions and validation. A recorded forest-area discrepancy is retained in the issue register. [COOK09](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.esd.2013.07.001), [COOK11](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2023.103524), [COOK12](https://doi.org/10.5772/intechopen.99365).

The waste study adds measured feedstock characteristics, while its methane kinetics remain calculated. That distinction matters when assessing biogas potential. [COOK19](https://doi.org/10.3390/su151914531).

**Research opportunity:** harmonize the survey baseline, then study fuel stacking, quantities, expenditure, cooking time, exposure and continued stove use across seasons. Electric cooking merits investigation, but a Somali-cuisine publication is not automatically a trial conducted in Somalia. See the [cooking review](contributions/cooking_review.md).

## Transport and petroleum: establish activity and fuel allocation

The official BUR describes a top-down transport approach constrained by missing vehicle activity and efficiency information. The transport starter kit includes Somalia, but transfers important regional assumptions. An environmental assessment also projects emissions from borrowed factors and assumed growth. These outputs should not be presented as direct measurements of Somali vehicles. [TF008](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/Somalia%20First%20BUR%20report%202022.pdf), [TF018](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.110984), [TF007](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/742491594100313982/pdf/Somalia-Country-Environmental-Analysis-Diagnostic-Study-on-Trends-and-Threats-for-Environmental-and-Natural-Resources-Challenges.pdf).

Urban mobility and political-economy research adds access, security, gender, displacement and institutional bargaining. These issues influence whether an apparently inexpensive energy intervention works for users. Petroleum governance studies do not verify commercially recoverable reserves. Policy discussion of electric vehicles likewise does not demonstrate operating-fleet savings. [TF009](https://riftvalley.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/rvi-commodified-cities-report-2022-en.pdf), [TF006](https://eprints.lse.ac.uk/107126/1/CRP_oil_and_gas_in_political_marketplace_somalia.pdf), [TF013](https://www.irena.org/-/media/Files/IRENA/Agency/Publication/2025/Jun/IRENA_COU_Energy_transition_assessment_Somalia_2025.pdf).

**Research opportunity:** reconcile fuel flows by end use, then measure activity for a bounded vehicle class or corridor. Compare electric options with efficient conventional vehicles, renewable charging and mobility improvements using costs, reliability, income and service access. No independently verified Somali EV fleet-performance evaluation entered this review; that is a search outcome, not proof of absence. See the [transport review](contributions/transport_review.md).

## Productive uses: study the service, not only the generator

The evidence spans irrigation, water supply, cold chains, firms, hospitals and schools. Hospital load data and the enterprise survey are promising access leads. Water research adds groundwater and maintenance constraints that a power-only optimization could miss. [PU01](https://doi.org/10.30501/jree.2021.245232.1140), [PU10](https://microdata.worldbank.org/catalog/8119), [PU05](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099615012012129914/pdf/P1749940c70d5d09008a3203ad9eeb97d1a.pdf), [PU13](https://evaluationreports.unicef.org/GetDocument?documentID=8933&fileID=35944).

Equipment provision is not equivalent to reliable refrigeration, lower spoilage or improved earnings. Fisheries, dairy and institutional assessments supply operational context, while several feasibility reports contain unresolved table or unit conflicts. Planned school systems must not be counted as commissioned generation. The desalination article has a correction whose content was not accessible. [PU06](https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2024-en-studie-somalia.pdf), [PU07](https://www.mfbe.gov.so/assets/documents/Fish%20Price%20Assessment%20Study%20%28Baseline%29%20Report.pdf), [PU15](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.22986.77762), [PU02](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10098-024-02741-1).

The Hobyo port paper adds pumped-storage modelling, but its site-specific elevation feasibility remains unestablished. Low simulated cost is not confirmation of constructibility. [PU18](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-96401-z).

**Research opportunity:** jointly observe energy and service outcomes—water volume/head/quality, cold-chain temperatures and throughput, clinical critical loads, business production and downtime. Evaluate maintenance, financing and access alongside system sizing. See the [productive-use review](contributions/productive_review.md).

## What this review changes for the project

1. **Do a targeted replication and data-access audit before expanding a model.** Existing models provide comparators, and their data dependencies help prioritize collection.
2. **Choose a decision that uncertainty can change.** Examples are whether to connect systems, which storage/service strategy to fund, or which household/vehicle transition remains affordable.
3. **Preserve distinct baselines.** Observed consumption, unmet service needs and future demand scenarios answer different questions. Geography, source year, units, price basis and measured/modelled status must travel with every input.
4. **Use the full energy review to frame research; scope implementations separately.** This review has not added sectors, run SWITCH or changed Kenya parameters.

These are reviewer recommendations. [Evidence gaps](EVIDENCE_GAPS.md), [data leads](DATA_LEADS.md), [source issues](SOURCE_ISSUES.md) and the [advisor brief](ADVISOR_BRIEF.md) make the next decision reviewable. The review has not pooled incompatible estimates, verified every underlying dataset or established exhaustive coverage.
