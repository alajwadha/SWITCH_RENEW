# Evidence gaps and research questions

These are judgments from the inspected corpus, not statements that no other studies exist. A data-access gap, a missing measurement and a research gap require different remedies.

| Area | Existing starting point | What remains missing or unresolved | Most useful next question |
|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity demand | SEAP utility aggregates, appliance models, hospital case | Reusable hourly series, outages, unmet service needs, customer classes and seasonal coverage | Which investments remain cost-effective when served demand and suppressed demand are both considered? |
| Assets and networks | Reports, project lists and geospatial planning | Reconciled operating status, units versus sites, network topology and losses | When is local hybridization preferable to connections between systems? |
| Renewable resources | Station studies, telecom PV case, global datasets | Raw observations, quality flags, multi-year validation and micrositing | How much does weather/model error alter storage and backup needs? |
| Cooking adoption | SIHBS/SHDS analyses, PROSCAL, market assessments | Harmonized fuels/weights; quantities, stacking, refills and sustained use | Which affordability/support arrangements produce durable adoption? |
| Cooking health and equity | Survey-derived risk and behavioral studies | Measured exposure, time use and distributional outcomes | Who receives benefits, and do modeled risk categories predict actual exposure? |
| Biomass supply | Kiln detection and forest mapping | Field conversion factors, regrowth, flows and competing land pressures | Can demand reduction be linked to measured woodland outcomes with uncertainty? |
| Transport fuels | BUR, environmental assessment, starter kit | Fleet/activity observations and reconciled allocation of imported fuels | Which modes dominate energy use under a transparent uncertainty account? |
| Electric mobility | Policy proposals and mobility context | Local operating trials, charging demand, financing and battery replacement | Which bounded fleet or route could improve costs, reliability and user income? |
| Irrigation and drinking water | Pump designs, hydrogeology and evaluations | Joint energy-flow/head records, sustainable abstraction, maintenance | When does solar pumping improve water service without worsening groundwater stress? |
| Fisheries, dairy and commerce | Market studies, centre assessments and WBES | Refrigeration duty, temperatures, spoilage, enterprise outcomes and informal firms | Which energy-service investment improves throughput and earnings in practice? |
| Health and education | Facility assessments, hospital modelling, planned systems | Verified commissioning, critical loads, outages and maintenance outcomes | Which service-reliability improvements justify storage and backup spending? |
| Governance and finance | Qualitative institutional work and sector assessments | Enforcement, actual financing terms, contract performance and tariff records | Which institutional arrangement sustains affordable, reliable service? |

## Questions already partly answered

Do not propose broad novelty claims such as “first national energy model,” “first hybrid-system feasibility study,” “first cooking-fuel determinants analysis,” or “first charcoal mapping.” The catalogue contains predecessors. A narrower claim requires an explicit comparison of location, population, data, method and outcome.

## Gaps in this review itself

- Not a subscription-database systematic review; search rankings are dynamic and some full texts are blocked.
- Eight entries use abstract-level inspection and six use official summaries; one of the latter is identity-only. Their findings cannot support a full methods audit.
- No comprehensive retraction/Crossmark audit was performed. DOI relationships were checked where available; the desalination correction is explicitly flagged.
- Arabic/Somali-language grey literature, theses, utility archives and unpublished evaluations may be underrepresented.
- Hydrogen, geothermal, ocean energy, aviation, shipping and industrial process heat did not receive enough directly inspected Somalia-specific material for a strong synthesis. This is a coverage limit and follow-up search priority, not proof of technical potential or research absence.
- Raw datasets, code dependencies and sample weights were not rerun; no causal meta-analysis or pooled LCOE estimate was attempted.

See the four search logs for actual queries, citation trails and inaccessible leads. Equal priority across topics does not require inventing equivalent evidence where verified coverage differs.
