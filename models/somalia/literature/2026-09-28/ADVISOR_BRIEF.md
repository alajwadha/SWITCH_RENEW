# Prepared checkpoint: Somalia energy research direction

For discussion with Dan Kammen; prepared 28 September2026. **No message or invitation has been sent.**

## Decision

Choose an initial research question and evidence-access strategy after a short replication/data audit. Keep the literature review broad; select implementation scope separately.

## Evidence that changes the framing

- National/spatial models and many local feasibility studies already exist. An unqualified “first Somalia energy model” claim is not defensible from this corpus.
- Important measured-data leads exist but are not yet reusable: utility records, hospital load/weather and telecom PV monitoring.
- Cooking and transport contain definition/proxy problems that change interpretation; productive-use studies often lack long-term service outcomes.
- Selected publications and reports contain numerical conflicts, so publication alone cannot qualify a calibration input.

Sources and exact limits are in [REVIEW](REVIEW.md), [DATA_LEADS](DATA_LEADS.md) and [SOURCE_ISSUES](SOURCE_ISSUES.md).

## Four candidate directions

| Direction | Decision-focused contribution | Evidence needed first |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity reliability and investment | Compare local hybrids and connections under demand, outage and financial uncertainty | Metered loads, operating assets, topology and commercial terms |
| Cooking affordability and durable use | Evaluate fuel/appliance transitions through seasonal use and subsidy changes | Harmonized survey baseline, quantities, refills, exposure and longitudinal follow-up |
| Transport energy and mobility | Build an uncertainty-aware fuel/activity account, then evaluate a bounded fleet transition | Workbook provenance, vehicle/activity sampling, charging and financial data |
| Productive energy services | Connect energy investment to reliable water, cold-chain or clinical service | Joint energy/service observations, maintenance records and suitable comparisons |

## Recommendation

Make data access the first selection criterion. Audit one existing model/data chain and assess the feasibility of obtaining observations for each candidate. For the SWITCH workbench, a bounded utility or city reliability study is a practical initial candidate **if** metered loads and operating-asset data are obtainable. Otherwise, a transparent evidence/proxy audit may be a stronger first output than a large simulation.

The decision should specify geography, baseline and horizon, service boundary, spatial/time resolution, comparator, validation target and uncertainty that could alter the conclusion. All additional sectors remain proposals until scoped. Kenya data are a checklist for required inputs, not default Somali parameter values.
