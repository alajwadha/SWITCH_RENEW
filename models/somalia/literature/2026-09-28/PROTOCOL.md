# Somalia energy literature review protocol

Review date: 28 September 2026. This is a structured, critical scoping review with a reproducible search record, not a claim of exhaustive systematic-review coverage.

## Authorized scope

The user requested literature and publications across **all Somalia energy topics equally**, including cooking fuels, transport and productive uses. The follow-up "1,2. AND MORE" is interpreted as permission to include both academic and institutional literature and provide a deep review with additional citation tracing, quality assessment, research gaps and GitHub publication. The review does not authorize new sector models or direct contact with authors/advisors.

## Review questions

1. What has been studied about electricity supply/access, renewable resources, grids/storage, cooking/biomass, transport/petroleum, productive uses, and energy governance/equity/climate policy in Somalia?
2. Which findings rest on measured Somali data, surveys, remote sensing, models, reviews, or policy/project documents?
3. What is geographically and methodologically transferable to future Somalia research, and what remains uncertain?
4. Which datasets or methods can fill the existing evidence gaps, and which proposed research contributions require a stronger novelty check?

## Search and screening

- Search academic discovery services and the web; follow results to publisher articles, DOI records, institutional repositories, original reports and official datasets. Discovery summaries and citation counts do not verify findings or quality.
- Use Somalia/Somali plus source-specific Somaliland, Puntland and named-city terms, preserving each source's geographic scope. Do not silently combine national and subnational results.
- Give electricity, cooking/biomass, transport/fuels and productive uses dedicated searches. Include governance, affordability, gender, displacement, climate, land/water and environmental consequences across those themes. Equal thematic attention does not mean inventing an equal number of papers where the accessible literature is thin.
- Search without an initial date cutoff through the review date. Retain older work when it supplies historical evidence or foundational methods, and distinguish publication year from data/reference years.
- Include peer-reviewed articles, conference papers and credible original government/development/research reports. Keep theses, preprints and publisher-status-uncertain papers separately labeled. Exclude off-topic country-name matches, duplicates and sources whose identity or findings cannot be checked.
- Trace useful references backward and relevant subsequent papers forward where feasible. Record actual search queries, platform, access date, results inspected and access limitations.
- Assess inaccessible publications from verified publisher abstracts only; explicitly label that level of review. Never report abstract-only access as a full-text methodological assessment.

## Record and appraisal

Each included item records a stable ID; exact title; authors or institution; publication year/type/venue; DOI when verified; primary URL; geography and data years; methods; data origins; compact finding; limitations; use for the present project; inspection depth and locator; evidence category; quality rationale; and linked-data availability. Unknown fields stay explicit.

Classify evidence as observed/survey, remote sensing, techno-economic simulation, statistical/econometric analysis, qualitative, review, policy/planning, or project/report evidence. A simulated optimum is not measured performance; association is not causation; publication or institutional reputation is not proof that a number is suitable for calibration.

Appraisal considers sample/coverage, measurement provenance, load/resource assumptions, comparison or validation, sensitivity analysis, reproducibility and geographic transfer. Do not infer quality from citation counts alone. Do not claim that all sources have been audited for corrections or retractions when that check was not performed.

## Synthesis and publication

Organize a publication table, concise thematic reviews, cross-cutting synthesis, evidence gaps, research opportunities and a recommended reading route. Link findings to included item IDs and precise sources. Keep data gaps separate from publication gaps and unresolved research questions. An unsuccessful search is not proof that no study exists.

Publish original notes, bibliographic metadata, scripts and compact provenance records in the existing Somalia research folder. Do not redistribute article/report PDFs, full abstracts or extracted full text without a verified license. Existing data snapshots and Kenya inputs remain unchanged.
